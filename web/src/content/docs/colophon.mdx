## Sources

- **Harvard Dialect Survey** (2003), Bert Vaux and Scott Golder — the published answer maps and state percentages.
- **Cambridge Survey of World Englishes** — the same question set, a later and differently recruited population.
- ***Pop vs Soda***, Alan McConchie — county-level returns used as an independent tuning target.
- **Yale Grammatical Diversity Project** — the only public source with real people and known raised locations.
- **US Census** — population, place names and the geographic prior.

## Method

Every number on this site is generated from <Stat n={content.inventory.length} /> data files by a single export step, so nothing here is typed by hand and nothing can drift from the analysis behind it.

The model runs in your browser; no answer is sent anywhere, there is no analytics on this site, and nothing you copy or save is recorded. That is also why the accuracy figures here are simulated rather than measured on the people who play the quiz — [the reason it was left that way](/american-dialects/limits/).

[Code, data and the full write-up](https://github.com/incrediblecrab/american-dialects) — including the findings this site summarises and the ones it does not.
